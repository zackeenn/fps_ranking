# 🎮 FPS Ranking Game! 🏆

Welcome to **FPS Ranking** — a simple and fun C program that ranks you and 9 other players based on your scores! Are you ready to see if you can become the champion? Let’s get started!

---

## ✨ Features

- **Randomized Competition:** Play against 9 randomly scored players.
- **Personalized Entry:** Enter your own player name and score!
- **Automatic Ranking:** See how you stack up with ascending (worst to best) and descending (best to worst) rankings.
- **Champion Announcement:** Find out who is the top scorer!

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Compile the Program**
   ```bash
   gcc fps_ranking.c -o fps_ranking
   ```

2. **Run the Game**
   ```bash
   ./fps_ranking
   ```

3. **Enter Your Name & Score**
   - You’ll be prompted to enter your player name and score.
   - Example: `Alex 275`

4. **Check the Rankings**
   - See your score among 9 other randomly generated players.
   - Rankings are shown in both ascending and descending order.
   - Find out if you’re the CHAMPION!
